# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Henry Company leadership, in a live meeting. They hear the case for why Tier 1 roofing contractors drift away from Henry and decide whether to fund and staff the fixes.

## Product Purpose
A PowerPoint deck (`Leaky_Bucket.pptx`) that shows the chronic, Henry-controlled problems that cost us Tier 1 roofer business ("leaky bucket" detractors) and the fixes for each. Success: leadership approves the fixes and names an owner for each leak.

## Positioning
Henry stops leaks for a living. The deck's argument is that our product, leads and pricing win Tier 1 roofers, while service, logistics, warranty speed, material readiness and lead volume leak them back out, and every one of those leaks is ours to fix.

## Operating Context
Presented live on a projector or screen share; the presenter narrates and speaker notes carry the detail. Source material is the team's "How to Get and Build Upon Tier 1 Roofer's Business" tree and the "Detractors – Leaky Bucket" tables.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Delivered as an editable .pptx that opens on standard Office installs, so fonts must be ones Office ships with.
- Status scale carried from the source deck: green = strength, yellow = watch, orange = at risk, red = broken (inferred from the source color coding).
- Open item: the moisture-survey fix reads only "John T Hull" in the source; its meaning is unconfirmed.

## Brand Commitments
Henry brand colors, taken from henry.com: Henry Blue #0054A6, navy #00306C, bright blue #0090D2, charcoal #333132, light gray #F1F1F2. Official Henry logo (white and blue variants). Henry is a Carlisle company.

## Evidence on Hand
Only the internal feedback and fixes in the source deck. No customer counts, churn figures, survey scores or direct customer quotes exist; do not invent them. The "one order" story is a composite of real feedback and is labeled as such.

## Product Principles
- Lead with the one idea: we win roofers on product and lose them on service.
- Concrete over abstract: real SKUs, plants and hours, not adjectives.
- Every problem ships with its fix and a measurable commitment.
- End on a decision, not a summary.
