# Surface brief: Leaky Bucket deck

Scope: `Leaky_Bucket.pptx`, the whole deck. Mode: Persuade (leadership decides and acts).

Audience and action: Henry leadership in a live meeting; approve the repairs and name an owner per leak.

Proof on hand: the detractor tables and the Tier 1 tree only. No invented metrics or quotes.

## Direction contract

1. World: a roof inspection report. Leaks are numbered callouts on a top-down roof plan, the way an inspector marks a moisture survey.
2. Hook: "We stop leaks for a living", the irony that a waterproofing company is leaking customers.
3. Motif: the roof plan with five red leak markers, opening the deck, mapping the leaks, and shrinking to a locator on every leak slide.
4. Palette: Henry Blue and navy carry the deck, white content slides for the projector, leak red used only for leaks and broken status.
5. Structure: hook → bucket → one-order story → leak map → five leaks (problem left, repair panel right) → what works → four numbers as a data-sheet table → decision slide with blank owner fields → appendix inspection results.
6. Memorable moment: the decision slide, where each leak gets an owner in the room.

Deliberate deviation: Henry's web faces (Proxima Nova, Praktika) are not on standard Office installs, so the deck uses Arial to stay editable and render correctly everywhere.
